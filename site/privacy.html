Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Short version: We don't collect, upload, store, sell or share your files or any personal information. Your documents are processed entirely inside your own web browser.

1. Who we are

This website ("TypeMyFile", "we", "us") is operated by Anthony Snyder]. You can contact me at ajfunnyhead@gmail.com.

2. Your files stay on your device

When you choose a file, your browser reads it locally to extract the text for the typing game. The file and its text are never sent to our servers or any third party. When you close or refresh the page, the text is gone.

3. Information we collect

  • No accounts, no forms, no tracking. We don't ask for your name, email or any other personal data.
  • No cookies or similar technologies. The site doesn't set cookies or use local storage, so there's no cookie banner.
  • No analytics or advertising. We don't use Google Analytics, ad networks, social media pixels or any third-party scripts.
  • Typing statistics (speed and accuracy) are calculated in your browser and are not recorded.

4. Server logs from our host

Like every website, our hosting provider ([HOSTING PROVIDER, e.g. Netlify / Cloudflare Pages / GitHub Pages]) may automatically record basic technical information when your browser requests a page, such as IP address, browser type and time of request, for security and to keep the service running. We don't use this to identify you. See the host's privacy policy for how long they keep it.

5. Legal basis (EU/UK users)

Because we don't process your personal data ourselves, most GDPR obligations don't come into play. Any server logs kept by our host are processed under legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR) to keep the website secure and working.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, or California's CCPA/CPRA), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to processing of your personal data, and to complain to your local data protection authority. Since we hold no personal data about you, there's normally nothing to access or delete, but you can always contact us with questions. We do not sell or share personal information.

7. Children

The site is suitable for all ages and doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (COPPA) or under 16 (GDPR).

8. Security

The site is served over HTTPS and uses a strict Content Security Policy so that only our own code can run on the page. Text from your documents is displayed as plain text and can't run as code.

9. Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page.

Accessibility statement

We aim to meet the WCAG 2.2 Level AA guidelines. The site works with a keyboard alone, respects your system's dark mode and reduced-motion settings, and labels its controls for screen readers. If something isn't accessible to you, please tell us at [YOUR CONTACT EMAIL].
